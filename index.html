About

My name is SENATOR OLOWOSAGO SAMSON, popularly known as SENATOR BIGGIE OF LAGOS.

I am from Ikorodu Local Government Area of Lagos State. I come from the family of Mr. & Mrs. Olowosago. My father's name is OLOWOSAGO BISORO OYEBOLA.

I am associated with student leadership, stakeholder engagement, youth development and public affairs activities in Lagos State.

Achievements & Impact

During his tenure as Director of Special Duties and Transport Affairs, NAUS CMC Lagos, the following activities and contributions are highlighted:

  • Stakeholder engagement with government, community and student stakeholders.
  • Official visits and engagements with public stakeholders.
  • Facilitated registration of over 30 eligible students for APC membership.
  • Supported 15 eligible students in obtaining their Permanent Voter's Cards (PVCs).
  • Strengthened communication between students, leaders and stakeholders.
  • Advocated for student transportation-related concerns.
  • Supported student representation in education, transportation, youth and community affairs.
  • Participated in youth development and public affairs engagement.
